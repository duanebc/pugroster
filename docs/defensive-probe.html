Defensive capture probe

Can PugRoster count the defensive cooldowns a group presses? Two runs of /pugdebug defprobe on a live Midnight client.

No — not for anyone but you.

Every one of 246 groupmate casts came back with a secret spell ID. All 459 of your own were readable. UNIT_SPELLCAST_SUCCEEDED fires for party members; the client simply will not say what they cast.

A curated spell list would therefore count only the player's own defensives, which is not a roster feature.

Still open

Whether groupmate auras are readable. The aura figures above counted globally without recording whose they were, so they are almost certainly the player's. The probe now scans eight aura slots per unit and reports per unit; one more grouped dungeon run settles it.
